Draw the shape of your board, with no size limit (the grid grows when you add blocks at the edge): each cell = one block of 10 steps (12.25 cm), the size of a vehicle. Blocks mode: tap an empty cell to add a block, a block to remove it (slide your finger to add several). The top-right corner block (fence and red stud, where calibration happens) always stays; the 2 red cells right behind its fences stay empty. Pinch with 2 fingers to zoom in / out and slide them to move the grid; ⤢ shows the whole board again. Unload mode: tap the block where vehicles will unload their marbles.
